Podcast Episode One-Sheet Template

A sponsor-ready one-pager for every episode.

A one-sheet is the fastest way to explain a podcast episode to a potential sponsor or guest. This template keeps it to one page: who, why, for whom, and what they get from joining.

  • Episode title, format, and run time
  • Guest or topic value in two sentences
  • Audience snapshot and why it matters to a sponsor
  • Sponsor deliverables: mentions, credits, social, and air dates
  • One contact route so every inquiry lands in the same place
