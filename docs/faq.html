Frequently asked questions about growing your brand.

Understand what we do, who we support, how monthly visibility works, and what you can expect before a campaign begins.

What services does Jaylen Sinegal provide?

Services include brand strategy and positioning, integrated marketing campaigns, content strategy and creative direction, digital and social media marketing, SEO and CRM optimization, sponsorship development and sales, community and partnership marketing, market research, business development, and event concept and coordination.

Who do you help?

Jaylen supports home-service businesses, professional experts, specialty businesses, car dealerships, boat rentals, RV rentals and parks, destinations, hospitality brands, and community-focused organizations.

What does monthly homeowner exposure mean?

Monthly exposure means building a consistent presence through selected print, digital, sponsorship, partnership, and community channels so your brand can stay familiar with high-value homeowners and families. The exact channels, audience, timing, and deliverables are defined in the campaign plan.

Do you work only in Broussard and Lafayette?

The primary local service area includes Broussard, Lafayette, Youngsville, and Acadiana. Strategies can also support businesses across Louisiana and nationwide through a multi-market network.

How do you help a business get found on Google?

The work can include search-focused messaging, local SEO structure, service and location relevance, content planning, CRM follow-up, and measurement. Rankings depend on competition, website quality, relevance, authority, and ongoing execution, so no ethical provider can guarantee a specific position.

What will I receive after a consultation?

The consultation identifies your audience, offer, visibility gaps, and practical next steps. Any recommended campaign includes clear goals, channels, deliverables, timing, and follow-up expectations before work begins.

How much does a campaign cost?

The minimum monthly investment is $90, and campaigns are not offered below that amount. The Signature Brand Offer includes 20% off the introductory month, with savings up to 40% available on select packages. Final pricing depends on your market, audience, channels, creative needs, and campaign scope.

Can I submit music for you to pitch or promote?

Music is not accepted for pitching or promotion through the general contact form. The Find a Producer directory remains in pre-launch, and no music submissions or membership payments are being accepted.
