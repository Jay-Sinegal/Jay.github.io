Final operating model

Ten boundaries that keep the directory focused.

  1. Producers purchase a $100 monthly directory membership.
  2. Producers create and maintain their own profiles.
  3. Producers submit only external links or authorized embeds.
  4. BeatStars, Airbit, SoundCloud, Audiomack, YouTube, or the producer’s website hosts the music.
  5. Visitors contact producers directly through producer-controlled destinations.
  6. Producers independently negotiate every license and sale.
  7. Producers receive 100% of their outside transaction proceeds.
  8. Sinegal receives only the monthly directory-membership fee.
  9. Sinegal may suspend or remove disputed material or profiles when appropriate.
  10. Sinegal never represents, verifies, sells, or licenses the music.
