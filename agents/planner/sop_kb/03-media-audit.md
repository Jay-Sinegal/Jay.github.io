# SOP 03 — Media Copyright and Facility Audit

Contact objective: "Media Copyright and Facility Audit".
For schools and organizations worried about DMCA strikes, unlicensed music on
highlight reels, or media use across athletic accounts.

## When this SOP applies
A school, AD, or org is asking about copyright exposure, takedowns, clearing
music, or reviewing media use - NOT booking a clinic. If the intent is booking
a clinic, use SOP 02.

## Audit scope (confirm before planning)
- Which accounts: school athletic accounts, social channels, YouTube.
- Where highlight films are hosted and who owns/controls each library.
- Whether media consent from parents is formalized for the footage used.
- Whether the media team reviews platform terms before posting athlete content.

## The DMCA checklist (education)
- School accounts that use commercial soundtracks on reels are exposed to
  takedowns and account-standing loss.
- Takedowns on highlight content can cost the content and hurt the account.
- The remedy taught is a search-confirm-license-release workflow, never
  "just delete it" or "rip audio".

## Master vs sync (core teaching)
- Master = the sound recording itself.
- Sync = the right to pair music with video.
- Commercial highlight reels normally need BOTH.
- Recommend cleared/royalty-free music that is actually licensed for school
  use; cite the quick-reference card the clinic provides.

## Deliverable
A written audit report (branded PDF) naming the gaps found, the DMCA
exposure level, and the ordered fixes. Keep it education: no claims of legal
authority, no solicitation for contract work.

## Optional tie-in
If the audit finds systemic gaps, recommend the Path B clinic (SOP 02) as an
education path: modules map 1:1 to the findings (music licensing module for
DMCA cases, agent-verification module for SB 389 fears). The audit is the
framer; the clinic is the fix - but never present the clinic as legal work.
