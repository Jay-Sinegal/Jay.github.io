# SOP 04 — FlightTime Athletics Combine and Training

Contact objective: "FlightTime Athletics Combine and Training Inquiry".
FlightTime Athletics LLC (Chief Brand Officer footprint). Youth physiological
speed and agility training, ages 8 to 17.

## Capabilities (from the public site and hub)
- Youth speed and agility training ages 8-17.
- BlazePod reactive light training and Vital Tanks sleds on the training floor.
- Standardized combine testing: 40-yard dash, 5-10-5 agility, vertical jump.
- AI metric leaderboards for measurable progress.
- Invitational "Flight Night" walkout showcases.

## Metrics as owned assets
- Every lookupable metric (times, marks, reaction scores, load progress)
  becomes proof the athlete owns and can license - "seen, measurable,
  remembered" equity.
- Standard set: 40-yard dash (standardized), 5-10-5 shuttle, broad jump,
  BlazePod reaction score, Vital Tanks sled push load progression.
- Baseline at intake, re-test at 8 weeks. Document both with media proof.

## Planning rules
- Combine/intake questions: route to FlightTime combine baseline + re-test.
- Training/membership questions: point to the FlightTime site
  (https://www.flighttimeathleticsllc.com). Membership pricing is handled by
  FlightTime directly - do not quote third-party numbers you cannot verify.
- Metric-documentation questions from families (e.g. "how do we record her
  speed for a college profile?") get the metrics-as-assets plan and can tie
  into Path A brand work only if the family asks for brand help too.
