# SOP 05 — Scheduling and Pricing

Rules that govern bookings, gates, and money questions. Applies to both paths.

## Scheduling - two event types only

### Path A: 1-on-1 Brand Architecture Consultation (paid)
- 60 minutes, payment captured at booking, Zoom or in-person.
- Bookable up to 2 hours before start; max 1 booking/day while solo.
- HARD GATE: you do not join the call until the Intake and Legal Boundary form
  is signed. Check 1 hour before. No exceptions. This is SB 389 insulation.
- Custom booking questions: athlete name; sport and graduation year; "ready to
  complete the Intake and Legal Boundary form before the call?"
- No-show after payment: one reschedule within 30 days; second no-show
  forfeits the fee (stated in the confirmation email).

### Path B: B2B Clinic Discovery Call (free)
- 20 minutes, Zoom or phone, bookable up to 24 hours ahead; max 3/day.
- HARD GATE: the call becomes a proposal only after the Athletic Department
  Risk Audit is submitted. On booking the AD receives the audit link.
- No-show: no penalty; reschedule. Late >10 minutes: prefer reschedule.

## Pricing questions
- Path A rate and Path B clinic investment are real, deliberately-set numbers
  recorded ONLY in `_OPERATIONS/03_PricingSheet` (private). Do not invent or
  doctor numbers in public copy.
- Payment is collected at booking for Path A; Path B discovery call is free and
  clinic proposals carry real pricing after the audit gate.
- FlightTime membership pricing is FlightTime's to publish (SOP 04); do not
  quote unverified third-party numbers.

## Planning rules for scheduling/pricing intents
- These are triage intents: confirm which path and event, enforce the matching
  gate reminder, and hand off the correct link (Calendly/Acuity, intake form,
  or audit form).
- Always needs_human: a person must confirm the gate before any call.
- Never let a scheduling or pricing question skip to a service plan on its
  own - it is support, not an engagement.
