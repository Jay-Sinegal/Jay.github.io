# SOP 01 — Path A: Executive Advisory and Brand Architecture

Contact objective: "Executive Brand Architecture (1-on-1 Consultation)".
For athletes and families. Education and media IP architecture only.

## Hard gate
No one-on-one Zoom call starts until the Intake and Legal Boundary Form is
submitted and signed electronically. Enforced at booking and checked 1 hour
before the call. No exceptions. This is the SB 389 insulation.

## Intake and Legal Boundary Form (the gate document)
- Captures athlete info (name, age, DOB, primary sport, graduation year,
  LHSAA member school?).
- Digital footprint: social handles, combined followers, highlight reel link,
  existing paid posts, existing NIL agreements, family understanding of them.
- Parent/guardian info (required when athlete is under 18).
- Expectations: goal, biggest concern, recruitment visibility vs media IP
  protection, timeline.
- Legal boundary checkboxes (wording fixed, required):
  1. Education in brand architecture and media IP; NOT a registered agent under
     SB 389; does not negotiate contracts.
  2. Education and strategy, not legal advice; rules can change.
  3. Parent/legal guardian completing for a minor.
- Electronic signature + date. Responses append to PathA_Intake_<MONTH-YEAR>.

## Deliverable: Prep Brand IP Audit (branded PDF)
Sections delivered to the family:
1. Social Media Scrub - LHSAA amateur status check. Flag any recruiting-motive
   or performance-incentive item; verify written parent consent exists for any
   existing agreement.
2. Platform Lock-In analysis - who owns the highlight library (Hudl, YouTube,
   Vimeo), who can revoke access, unlicensed songs on reels (DMCA exposure),
   backup outside the platform. Outcome: athlete controls independent proof.
3. FlightTime AI tracking milestones - 40-yard dash, 5-10-5 shuttle, broad
   jump, BlazePod reaction score, Vital Tanks sled progression, combine
   baseline vs 8-week re-test. Lookupable metrics become owned, licensable
   assets.
4. Digital equity snapshot - asset counts, owned proof, amateur-status risk,
   90-day plan.
5. Recommended next steps checklist (family-controlled).
Footer mandated: "Education, not representation..." Not legal advice.

## Consultation deck: "The Architecture"
10 slides: cover, boundary (mandatory slide 2), why before college, Louisiana
changed the rules (Act 810/SB 389), audit results, proof of work, three-layer
architecture (own proof, scrub for safety, build shareable assets), 90-day
roadmap, deliverables, close. Per-client copy; never bleed names between
clients. Media IP education only.

## Booking rules
1-on-1 Brand Architecture Consultation, 60 min, paid at booking, Zoom or
in-person, bookable max 1/day while solo. Custom questions: athlete name,
sport + graduation year, readiness to sign the form before the call.
