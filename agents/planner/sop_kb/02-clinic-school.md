# SOP 02 — Path B: Sports IP and NIL Clinics for Schools

Contact objective: "School / Organization NIL and Music Licensing Clinic".
For high schools, athletic directors, districts, and clubs. Compliance
education for Act 810 and SB 389 plus music licensing education.

## Hard gate
A B2B Discovery Call does not become a B2B Executive Proposal until the
Athletic Department Risk Audit is submitted. The audit frames the clinic as a
necessity: problem-solving before presenting the solution.

## Athletic Department Risk Audit (the gate form)
- School/contact: name, parish, AD name/email/phone, varsity sport count,
  student-athlete count, classification.
- Act 810 section: written NIL policy? compliance point of contact? written
  parent consent process? tracking of consent? athlete/family NIL questions?
  signed agreements to knowledge?
- SB 389 section: outside people contacting athletes? system to verify AG
  registration? SB 389 awareness shared? watch-labels used ("advisor",
  "recruiting consultant", "brand manager", "agent").
- Media/music: commercial music in reels? cleared music? master vs sync
  knowledge? DMCA takedowns? who manages social accounts?
- Highlights: where hosted (Hudl, YouTube, school website, X)? independent
  athlete access? formalized media consent? platform terms reviewed?
- Priority: 1-10 confidence, best clinic slot, biggest question, timeline.
Responses feed PathB_RiskAudits_<MONTH-YEAR>. The audit is structured so at
least one answer lands on "unsure" or "no"; those gaps open the discovery call.

## B2B Executive Proposal (one-page PDF)
Header: official Louisiana Storyteller + credentials. Title: "A 2-Hour
On-Campus Compliance Clinic Before the Rules Cost You a Season". Problem =
Act 810 + SB 389 obligations. Solution = 2-hour clinic, education only.
What the program walks away with: Act 810 parental-consent template, SB 389
agent-verification checklist, LHSAA amateur-status guidelines + media scrub
checklist, master vs sync quick reference, coach/parent talking points.
Credibility: Storyteller designation, coaching credential, music business
degree, published Act 810 analysis on jaylensinegal.com. Pricing placeholders.
Next step: book the discovery call. Footer: boundary statement.

## Clinic deck: "Safe-Harbor" (2 hours, 120 total)
- Module 1 (30 min): LHSAA Amateur Status under Act 810 - what it gives
  (written consent, NIL policy, compliance POC, jersey equity) and prohibits
  (enrollment-contingent compensation, performance incentives).
- Module 2 (25 min): Spotting Predatory Agents under SB 389 - who must
  register, enforcement (athlete can sue, forfeiture), verification checklist,
  red flags.
- Module 3 (30 min): Sports Music Licensing - Master vs Sync, reels usually
  need BOTH, DMCA strike risk on school accounts, where cleared music lives.
- Q&A (20 min) + action packet handoff (15 min).
Closing: retainer - "This Should Be Annual"; follow up within 2 business days.

## Booking rules
B2B Clinic Discovery Call, 20 min, free, Zoom or phone. On booking the AD
receives the audit link. After the clinic, yearly refresh offer.

## Media audit overlap
Schools asking about DMCA strikes, music on reels, or media use reviews cross
into SOP 03 (Media Audit). Keep those planning steps in SOP 03 and pull the
audit-based steps here only when the intent is a clinic booking.
