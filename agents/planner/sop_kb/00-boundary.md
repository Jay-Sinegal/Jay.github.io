# SOP 00 — The Boundary (retrieve for EVERY inquiry)

Non-negotiable facts that guide every plan. Applies to all departments.

- Jaylen Sinegal provides brand strategy, media education, and IP consulting.
  He is NOT a licensed sports agent and does NOT solicit, negotiate, or procure
  athletic contracts or employment.
- Louisiana disclaimer that must appear in all materials: "Education, not
  representation. Not a licensed sports agent."
- SB 389 / Act 895 (in force June 9, 2026): anyone acting as an athlete agent
  must register with the Louisiana Attorney General, pass a background check,
  complete AG-prescribed training, and (nonresidents) appoint the Secretary of
  State for service of process. Registration deadline passed August 1, 2026.
- Act 810 (HB 513): extends NIL rights to interscholastic athletes; requires
  written parent/legal-guardian consent before a minor signs any NIL agreement;
  prohibits compensation contingent on enrollment or specific athletic
  performance; schools must adopt NIL policies and name a compliance point of
  contact.
- Never negotiate contracts. Educate on media IP, brand architecture, and
  licensing. Refer the family or school to qualified professionals for legal
  advice.
- Any message seeking an agent, a negotiated contract, account management, or
  compensation procurement is OUT_OF_SCOPE and must be declined, never served.
- This work is education, not legal advice. Louisiana NIL rules can change.
