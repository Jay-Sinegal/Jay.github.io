## Slide 4 — The Landscape (Louisiana context)
- Headline: Louisiana Changed the Rules
- Act 810: high school athletes can build NIL value; written parent/guardian
  consent required; no recruiting-motive or performance-incentive deals
- SB 389/Act 895: agents must register with the LA Attorney General; education
  and representation are different lanes
- LHSAA amateur status hangs on compliance
