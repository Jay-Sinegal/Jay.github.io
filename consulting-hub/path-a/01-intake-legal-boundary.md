## Why this insulates you (SB 389 context)

SB 389 (Act 895), in force June 9, 2026, requires athlete agents to register with
the Louisiana Attorney General, complete mandated training, pass a background
check, and for nonresidents, appoint the Secretary of State for service of
process. Providing education in brand architecture and media IP — with an
explicit, signed boundary that you do NOT negotiate contracts — keeps this
engagement squarely in the education lane, not the registered-agent lane. The
checkbox forces the family to acknowledge in writing before the work begins.
