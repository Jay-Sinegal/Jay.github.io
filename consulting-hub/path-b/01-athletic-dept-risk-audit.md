# PATH B — Document 1: The Athletic Department Risk Audit (Google Form)

**Platform:** Google Form. Sent to Athletic Directors before clinic booking.
**Purpose:** Diagnose vulnerabilities created by Act 810 and SB 389 so the
clinic is framed as a necessity, not a nice-to-have (problem-solving before
presenting the solution).
**Gate rule:** The B2B Discovery Call does not become a proposal until the AD
completes this audit.

---

## Form header

**Title:** Athletic Department NIL and Media Compliance Risk Audit

**Description:**
> Louisiana's new rules changed what every school must prove. Act 810 governs
> high school NIL rights, parent consent, and amateur status. SB 389 (Act 895)
> governs who can act as an athlete agent in Louisiana and how they register
> with the Attorney General. This 5-minute audit maps where your program stands
> today so we can talk specifics on a discovery call. Completing it does not
> obligate your school to anything. It simply shows your blind spots before a
> problem finds them first.

## Sales framing (how this becomes the clinic pitch)

The audit is structured so at least one answer lands on "unsure" or "no".
Those answers become the opening of the discovery call:
- "You said no system verifies SB 389-registered agents. That single gap is
  what the clinic's module three resolves."
- The 2-hour on-campus clinic (B2B proposal) is the natural next step after
  the audit exposes the gap. Do not present the clinic before the audit is
  submitted; the discovery call is where the necessity is confirmed together.
