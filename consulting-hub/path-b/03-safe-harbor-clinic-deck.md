## MODULE 1 — LHSAA Amateur Status under Act 810 (30 min)

## Slide 4 — What Act 810 Gives You
- Interscholastic athletes can leverage NIL (subject to LHSAA amateur status)
- Written parent/guardian consent required before a minor signs anything
- Schools must adopt NIL policies and designate a compliance point of contact
- Jersey/apparel sales stay equivalent across the team; no exclusive
  school-brand vesting

## Slide 5 — What Act 810 Prohibits
- Compensation contingent on enrollment at a particular school
- Compensation for specific athletic performance or recruiting motive
- Money for merely being an athlete (must be for actual promotion/appearance)
- Unwritten deals that bypass parental consent

## Slide 6 — The Amateur Status Check
- Interactive walk-through: three example scenarios, ask the room to vote
- Each ends with: safe / needs consent / amateur-status risk
- Tool: the written-consent checklist they leave with

## MODULE 2 — Spotting Predatory Agents (SB 389 education, 25 min)

## Slide 7 — Who Must Register
- SB 389 / Act 895 in force since June 9, 2026
- Anyone acting as an athlete agent must register with the Louisiana Attorney
  General, pass a background check, complete AG-prescribed training, and
  (nonresidents) appoint the Secretary of State for service of process
- Registration deadline passed August 1, 2026; annual fee + renewal

## Slide 8 — Enforcement Matters to You
- Unregistered agents can be sued by the athlete and may forfeit compensation
- Athletic departments are the front line for families
- Ask the room: "Who in this room can verify an 'advisor' is registered?"

## Slide 9 — The Verification Checklist (teachable)
- The exact steps anyone can take to check AG registration status
- Watch-labels list: "advisor", "recruiting consultant", "brand manager"
- Red flags: urgency, pressure for percentage, bypassing parents

## MODULE 3 — Sports Music Licensing (Master vs Sync, 30 min)

## Slide 10 — Why Music Is a Compliance Landmine
- Highlights with commercial soundtracks = copyright exposure on school
  accounts
- DMCA strikes can cost content and account standing

## Slide 11 — Master vs Sync (Music Business 101)
- Master: the sound recording itself
- Sync: the right to pair music with video
- Reels typically need BOTH for commercial use
- Include: where to find cleared/royalty-free music that is actually licensed
  for school use

## Slide 12 — The Safe Media Flow
- If it is not licensed, it does not ship
